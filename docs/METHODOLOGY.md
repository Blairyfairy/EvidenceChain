# Evidence methodology

## Classification
- **Established**: publicly documented history or independently replicated phenomenon within stated bounds.
- **Reported**: attributed journalism or disclosures not necessarily independently validated.
- **Assessed**: a government or expert judgment with uncertainty and competing assessments.
- **Unverified**: missing independent, replicable demonstration of the specific claim.

## Non-transitivity
MKULTRA historical intent + RF auditory effect + satellite communication **does not entail** satellite-delivered covert speech, arbitrary thought decoding, or an operational targeting program. Separate intent, physical effect, engineering feasibility, deployment evidence, and attribution.

## Tests needed for stronger claims
1. Pre-register precise measurable outputs and acceptable error rates.
2. Measure under blinded, independently monitored conditions.
3. Establish mechanism, dose/exposure conditions, instrumentation and reproducibility.
4. Rule out ordinary audio, expectation effects, instrumentation artifacts and other confounds.
5. Replicate with independent labs and share anonymized data and analysis code.
6. For deployment claims, authenticate records and establish attribution independently.

## Research boundaries
Use public records, synthetic signals, and consented openly licensed research data. Never collect private communications, identify purported targets, or construct neural influence hardware.
